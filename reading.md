# CELPIP-General Reading

The four scored parts have 38 questions. CELPIP's Reading Pro study pack gives approximate part timers of 11, 9, 10, and 13 minutes. These add up to the 43 minutes in PipPrep's full mock. CELPIP's current format page gives a total Reading window of 43–56 minutes and describes unscored items that may appear alongside scored material. The four official sample screens opened at 11, 9, 10, and 13 minutes in Chrome. PipPrep presents only the four scored parts. ## Marking and feedback

Reading is computer-scored. Each correct response earns one point, and wrong or blank answers earn zero. The official test equates forms by item difficulty. The published raw-score ranges overlap and group levels 10–12 together. PipPrep shows every matching reference band for a full mock, so 33/38 displays 9 or 10–12, and 38/38 displays 10–12. Scores on single-part drills are reported as raw points. [Official Reading score ranges](https://www.celpip.ca/take-celpip/test-results/)

Every practice part has a fixed key, an explanation per question, and supporting quotes. For an E answer in Part 3, the explanation identifies what the claim adds beyond the closest related passage. The generator is prompted to solve and check each item before returning it. PipPrep validates the part structure, blank placement, option counts, and exact source evidence before a generated test opens. These checks catch malformed items and fabricated quotes. Generated item difficulty and answer quality can still vary.

AI coaching uses the scored choices, missed skills, unanswered questions, and pacing to suggest what to practise next. The review shows the original source and every correct answer even if the coaching request fails.
